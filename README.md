# Super Store Sales Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard analysing sales, profit, shipping and customer behaviour for a US retail store (2019-2020).

## Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end sales analysis built in Power BI. The dashboard gives management a single view of revenue, profit, delivery performance and customer preferences, and allows filtering by region to support data-driven decisions.

## Business Objectives
- Identify the regions, segments and products that drive sales and profit.
- Track monthly sales and profit trends and compare 2019 with 2020.
- Evaluate shipping modes and average delivery time.
- Understand customer payment preferences.

## Dashboard Features
- **KPI cards:** Total Sales, Profit, Orders and Average Ship Days.
- **Region slicer:** Central, East, South and West buttons filter every visual.
- **Composition charts:** Sales by Region, Segment and Payment Mode.
- **Trend analysis:** Monthly Sales and Monthly Profit, compared by year.
- **Product analysis:** Sales by Category and Top 3 Sub-Categories.
- **Operations:** Sales by Ship Mode.
- **Geographic view:** Sales and Profit by State on a map.

## Key Insights
- Total sales reached **$1.6M** with a profit of **$175K**, a margin of roughly **11%**.
- The store handled **3,003 orders**, an average of about **$530 in sales per order**.
- **West (33%)** and **East (29%)** contribute about 62% of total sales, while **South (16%)** is the weakest region.
- The **Consumer** segment leads with **48%** of sales, followed by Corporate (33%) and Home Office (19%).
- **Office Supplies** is the top category ($0.64M), followed by Technology ($0.47M) and Furniture ($0.45M).
- The top three sub-categories are **Phones ($196.6K)**, **Chairs ($181.9K)** and **Binders ($175.0K)**.
- **Standard Class** accounts for the largest share of sales ($0.91M), and average shipping time is **3.93 days**.
- **Cash on Delivery (43%)** is the most used payment mode, ahead of Online (35%) and Cards (22%).
- Sales in **2020 are higher than 2019** in most months, peaking around **September** and **December**.
- Monthly profit is volatile and does not always follow sales, which points to margin pressure in certain months.

## Recommendations
1. Strengthen the South region through targeted promotions, as it trails all other regions.
2. Plan inventory ahead of the peak season for top sub-categories such as Phones, Chairs and Binders.
3. Investigate profit volatility by reviewing discounts and costs in months where profit falls despite strong sales.
4. Promote prepaid payment options to reduce reliance on Cash on Delivery.
5. Review premium shipping options, since they generate far less revenue than Standard Class.
